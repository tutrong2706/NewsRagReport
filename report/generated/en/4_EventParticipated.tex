opportunity to participate in technology events and workshops organized
by AWS. These were valuable opportunities to update my knowledge on
Cloud, GenAI, and expand my network in the IT industry.

Below are some highlight events I attended and the lessons learned that
were directly applied to the NewsRAG project:

integrated with Generative AI, which helped me better understand

them to the NewsRAG system.
