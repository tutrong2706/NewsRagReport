\hypertarget{rag-evaluation-with-ragas}{%
\subsection{2. RAG Evaluation with
RAGAS}}

\hypertarget{why-ragas}{%
\subsubsection{Why RAGAS?}}

RAGAS evaluates RAG systems using LLM-as-judge across 4 metrics:

\begingroup
\small
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{3pt}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.15}
\begin{longtable}[]{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule\noalign{}
Metric & What it measures & Target \\
\midrule\noalign{}
\endhead
\bottomrule\noalign{}
\endlastfoot
\textbf{Faithfulness} & Answer grounded in retrieved context &
\textgreater{} 0.8 \\
\textbf{Answer Relevancy} & Answer addresses the question &
\textgreater{} 0.8 \\
\textbf{Context Precision} & Relevant chunks ranked higher &
\textgreater{} 0.7 \\
\textbf{Context Recall} & All relevant chunks retrieved & \textgreater{}
0.7 \\
\end{longtable}
\endgroup
