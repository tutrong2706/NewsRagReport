\hypertarget{crawler-development}{%
\section{Crawler Development}}

This section covers building the Scrapy-based news crawler that extracts
articles from Vietnamese news sites using SitemapSpider.

\hypertarget{why-sitemapspider}{%
\subsection{Why SitemapSpider?}}

Unlike traditional \texttt{CrawlSpider} with \texttt{DEPTH\_LIMIT},
\texttt{SitemapSpider} reads \texttt{sitemap\_news.xml} directly,
ensuring: - \textbf{No missed articles} --- discovers all URLs in
sitemap, not just linked pages - \textbf{Historical access} --- can
crawl old articles via sitemap archives - \textbf{Faster discovery} ---
no need to follow pagination links - \textbf{Respects robots.txt} ---
built-in \texttt{ROBOTSTXT\_OBEY\ =\ True}

\hypertarget{adding-new-news-sites}{%
\subsection{Adding New News Sites}}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Analyze the site} --- Find \texttt{sitemap\_news.xml} (usually
  at \texttt{/sitemap\_news.xml} or \texttt{/sitemap.xml})
\item
  \textbf{Inspect article pages} --- Use browser DevTools to find XPath
  selectors for title, content, author, date, category
\item
  \textbf{Add to config\_site.json} --- Copy an existing entry and
  modify selectors
\item
  \textbf{Test locally} --- Run spider and verify extraction
\item
  \textbf{Deploy} --- Rebuild Docker image, push to ECR
\end{enumerate}
