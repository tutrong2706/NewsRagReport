\hypertarget{key-highlights}{%
\subsection{3. Key Highlights}}

\hypertarget{the-agentic-ai-breakthrough-when-ai-autonomous-plans-and-acts}{%
\subsubsection{The Agentic AI Breakthrough: When AI Autonomous Plans and
Acts}}

Agentic AI marks a revolutionary paradigm shift where AI is no longer
merely passively responding. Instead, it has the capability to
autonomously plan, invoke business tools/APIs to solve complex problems,
and independently verify results.

This was vividly demonstrated through the KFC Bot Agent---a system that
helps customers order food directly via Zalo or Messenger without
switching apps or dealing with tedious registrations. Even more
impressive was the SA Professional app, which could automatically
extract Business Requirements Document (BRD) details, draw architectural
diagrams on Draw.io, and estimate AWS infrastructure costs within
minutes.

\hypertarget{the-24-hour-hackathon-journey-chaotic-but-invaluable}{%
\subsubsection{The 24-Hour Hackathon Journey: Chaotic but
Invaluable}}

The hackathon was truly a sweet and emotional ``trial by fire,'' taking
the teams through an entire spectrum of emotions: from deep anxiety and
total energy depletion to the exhilarating moment when the product
finally worked.

The ``laugh-or-cry'' behind-the-scenes stories---internal arguments over
undefined roles, staying awake until 3 AM debugging code, chugging 5
cans of Red Bull, forgetting to commit code, or accidentally pushing
secret files (\texttt{.env}) to GitHub---left unforgettable lessons that
no school or textbook could ever teach.

\hypertarget{key-takeaways}{%
\subsection{4. Key Takeaways}}

\hypertarget{the-pragmatic-mvp-mindset}{%
\subsubsection{The Pragmatic MVP
Mindset}}

``Small, finished work beats big, broken ideas.'' This is the most
valuable lesson for overcoming time pressure: having the courage to trim
features and focusing entirely on executing one core feature flawlessly
(``Scope it tiny'').

To deliver a successful 3-minute pitch to the judges, clear role
division (who codes, who designs, who pitches) and meticulously
preparing the demo script proved to be the decisive factors.

\hypertarget{flexible-and-cost-efficient-architecture}{%
\subsubsection{Flexible and Cost-Efficient
Architecture}}

A smart system design is one that allows the product to change and scale
without having to be completely rebuilt. This is achieved by leveraging
adapters to connect communication channels and decoupling independent
logic-processing tools.

Applying frameworks like AgentCore not only helped slash infrastructure
source code by up to 60\% but also maintained extremely economical
operating costs (only \$0.006 per order via KFC Bot, or leveraging
highly elastic serverless resources for cost optimization like Project
Signal Scout).

\hypertarget{the-power-of-community-and-team-diversity}{%
\subsubsection{The Power of Community and Team
Diversity}}

The greatest victory after a hackathon does not lie in the physical
prizes, but in the teammates who fought shoulder-to-shoulder through the
night and the lasting relationships forged within the community.

Diversity in individual strengths within a team (differing skill sets
are always better than uniform skill sets) is the key to solving
multi-dimensional problems under crushing time pressure.

\hypertarget{applying-to-work}{%
\subsection{5. Applying to Work}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Apply the ``Scope it tiny'' Philosophy}: Always prioritize
  completing small product versions (MVPs) decisively to gather
  real-world user feedback, avoiding the trap of designing massive but
  perpetually unfinished features.
\item
  \textbf{Build Agent-Based Architectures}: Design highly modular
  systems, allowing for easy plug-and-play integration of new automation
  tools or distribution channels without disrupting the core source
  code.
\item
  \textbf{Proactive Cost Management}: Deeply research cloud cost
  optimization methods based on actual resource measurement. Actively
  use cost estimation tools (AWS Pricing API/Calculator) right from the
  architectural sketching phase.
\item
  \textbf{Prepare the Launchpad}: Always proactively prepare starter
  templates, cloud accounts, and necessary tools to dramatically
  accelerate deployment speed whenever a new project suddenly arises.
\end{itemize}

\hypertarget{lessons-learned}{%
\subsection{7. Lessons Learned}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Never wait until you feel ``completely ready'' before daring to sign
  up for practical arenas. The courage to step out of your comfort zone
  is the greatest launchpad.
\item
  An outstanding AI Agent system needs more than just a smart Large
  Language Model (LLM); more importantly, it requires the ability to
  connect precisely with a company's reliable internal data to take
  practical actions.
\item
  Learn to love the chaos, the midnight debug errors, and the
  ``imperfect'' products. Because that is precisely where the most
  profound lessons and priceless memories of an engineering life are
  kept.
\end{itemize}
