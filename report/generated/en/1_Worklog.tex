Throughout \textbf{8 weeks} of internship in the \textbf{First Cloud AI
Journey} program (from June 1, 2026 to August 14, 2026), I participated
in building the \textbf{NewsRAG} project --- an intelligent news
aggregation and analysis system based on RAG (Retrieval-Augmented
Generation) architecture, fully deployed on AWS.

My primary role in the team was to develop the \textbf{Crawl + Queue}
module, including: writing Scrapy Spiders to crawl news from 3
Vietnamese news outlets, packaging Docker containers for the Fargate
Crawler, configuring SQS + Dead Letter Queue, and deploying to ECR/ECS.
