Throughout the First Cloud AI Journey internship program, I had the opportunity to participate in technology events and workshops organized by AWS. These were valuable opportunities to update my knowledge on Cloud, GenAI, and expand my network in the IT industry.

Below are some highlight events I attended and the lessons learned that were directly applied to the NewsRAG project:

### [1. GenAI-powered App-DB Modernization Workshop](4.1-Event1/)
An in-depth workshop on modernizing applications and databases integrated with Generative AI, which helped me better understand Serverless architecture, Event-Driven Architecture, and how to apply them to the NewsRAG system.

### [2. AWS Serverless & Container Day](4.2-Event2/)
An event focused on container technologies (ECS, EKS, Fargate) and serverless (Lambda, EventBridge). The knowledge from this event directly helped the team decide to use ECS Fargate for the Crawler instead of Lambda to avoid timeout limits.
